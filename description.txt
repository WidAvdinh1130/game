Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two
armchairs. Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along
with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting. You
can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good
vacuuming.
Doorways lead west to your father's office, north to your
bedroom, and east to the kitchen.

Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here
when you were six. A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next
to your twin bed. Posters of various anime characters cover one
wall. A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentience,
occupies the far corner.
The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of
clothes. You can return south to the living room.
